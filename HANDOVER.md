# Editor handover outline

The WordPress CMS is planned and has not been provisioned for this prototype. The public site's `/admin` route will open the WordPress dashboard after `WORDPRESS_ADMIN_URL` is configured. WordPress owns login and content permissions. These steps describe the editorial workflow to implement and verify before client handover.

## Publish an article
1. Sign in to the client-owned WordPress admin account.
2. Open **Articles → Add new**. Enter the title, summary, body, language and optional translated-article link.
3. Assign the approved author, one main section, and relevant tags. Add licensed imagery and alt text where needed.
4. Preview on desktop and mobile. Check attribution, links and spelling.
5. Save as a draft for editor review. An editor approves and schedules or publishes it.

## Edit a page or author
Open **Pages** or **Authors**, make the change, preview it, and publish the revision. Author profiles should include a biography and all approved article links.

## Manage sections and tags
Use **Articles → Categories** for the magazine's five main sections. Use **Tags** for specific themes. Avoid creating near-duplicate tags; keep a small editorial vocabulary.

## Review a submission
Open the private **Submissions** queue. Check the manuscript and publication consent, record the review decision, and contact the writer. For accepted work, create a separate draft article and assign the approved author. Submissions never become public automatically. Follow the agreed retention and withdrawal policy for files.

## Routine care
Review backups, WordPress updates, form delivery, newsletter signup and analytics regularly. The final handover should include account owners, recovery access, hosting details and a recorded walkthrough with screenshots from the actual CMS.
