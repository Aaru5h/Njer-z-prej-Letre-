# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Stack
Next.js front end with headless WordPress for production, as requested by the project owner in response to the client's WordPress preference. The current build is a prototype.

## Users
Albanian-speaking readers looking for substantive literature and cultural writing; emerging and diaspora writers seeking a considered editorial platform; editors reviewing and publishing contributions.

## Product Purpose
Njerëz prej Letre — Marginalia is an independent literary and cultural magazine. It publishes writing on literature, philosophy, psychology, spirituality, identity, and the human experience, while making room for underrepresented Albanian voices. Readers should discover and read work; writers should understand how to submit; editors should control what is published.

## Positioning
An Albanian-first magazine that joins literary work to open, critical conversation and gives emerging and diaspora writers editorial visibility.

## Operating Context
The editorial team curates articles, author profiles, categories, and submissions. A submission is reviewed before publication. Albanian is the initial language, with English translations anticipated.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Sections: Home, Literature, Emerging Voices, The Diaspora, Literary Criticism, Ideas & Reflections, About, Submit/Contact.
- Search, categories, tags, author pages, article pages, submission intake, newsletter subscription, Instagram access, SEO, analytics, accessibility, and mobile support are required for launch.
- Uploaded manuscripts or links must be accepted, and contributions must never publish automatically.
- Domain, hosting, accounts, and assets must be under client control.
- Newsletter provider, analytics, hosting, budget, and publication policies remain open decisions.

## Brand Commitments
The central name is “Njerëz prej Letre,” with “Marginalia” as subtitle. The client asks for an intimate, independent editorial character, warm muted colors, serif headlines, readable body text, whitespace, and literary details. Primary language is Albanian.

## Evidence on Hand
Only the client brief supplied in this conversation. No approved logo, photography, article copy, author names, social account, or integration credentials were supplied. Any launch content in the local prototype is illustrative.

## Product Principles
- Give writing and authors room to breathe.
- Encourage inquiry and disagreement without superficial promotion.
- Make emerging and diaspora voices easy to discover.
- Keep editorial control over publication.
- Prepare for bilingual material without redesigning the information architecture.
